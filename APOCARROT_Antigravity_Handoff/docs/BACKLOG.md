# APOCARROT — Feature Backlog (not part of P0)

This file collects ideas that must not interrupt the first playable demo. Items are ideas only, not commitments.

## Later candidates
- Additional weapon types: spear, axe variants, lemon poison, watermelon burst, cherry multi-target, coconut knockback.
- More weapon evolution levels and combination recipes.
- Additional rabbit heroes, skins and character-specific progression.
- More worlds/biomes and a world map with many stages.
- Codex/collection for enemy types and weapons.
- Daily missions, advanced achievements and long-term challenge modes.
- Endless mode and boss rush.
- Base/home upgrades.
- More polished 3D rigs, animation sets, VFX and sound design.
- Energy store or monetization, only after core game is validated and business scope is explicitly approved.
- Cloud save, account backend, leaderboard, multiplayer/co-op/PvP.
